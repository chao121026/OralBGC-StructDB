PHRC_BGCStructDB

A structure-enabled biosynthetic gene cluster protein resource for oral microbiome MAGs

This public database links MAG-derived BGCs and BiG-SCAPE GCFs to encoded proteins, AlphaFold3 models, AF3 quality metrics, compactness descriptors, and Foldseek/PDB structural annotations.

Why This Resource Is Different

PHRC_BGCStructDB emphasizes protein-level structure context for oral microbiome MAG-associated BGCs. The distinctive layer is the linkage from each BGC protein to predicted structures, model quality, compactness, and structural similarity categories.

  • MAG-resolved oral microbiome context
  • Protein-level BGC annotation
  • Downloadable CIF structures
  • AF3 confidence and compactness metrics
  • Systematic Foldseek/PDB comparison
