Research Context

The oral microbiome contains extensive biosynthetic and protein structural diversity with potential relevance to microbial ecology, host-microbe interactions, and natural-product research. {{ brand.public_name }} integrates metagenome-assembled genomes, antiSMASH-predicted biosynthetic gene clusters, BiG-SCAPE gene cluster families, encoded proteins, AlphaFold 3 models, and structural-similarity annotations in a unified resource.

  • MAG-resolved oral microbiome context
  • Protein-level BGC annotation
  • Downloadable CIF structures
  • AF3 confidence and compactness metrics when available
  • Systematic Foldseek/PDB comparison when available

Data Layer Boundaries

Structure availability means an approved CIF file can be served. AF3 QC and Foldseek/PDB annotation are separate release layers and are counted independently throughout the site.
