About / Citation

PHRC_BGCStructDB_v1 is a focused oral microbiome MAG-resolved BGC-protein resource with AF3 model, AF3 QC, compactness, and Foldseek/PDB structural annotation layers.

Comparison

BGC Atlas is a broad environmental metagenomic BGC/GCF atlas. ABC-HuMi is a human microbiome BGC atlas spanning multiple body sites. PHRC_BGCStructDB focuses on oral microbiome MAG BGC proteins and downloadable CIF structures.

Maintainer and institutional details are placeholders for the public release.
