About / Citation

PHRC_BGCStructDB is a focused oral microbiome MAG-resolved BGC-protein resource that links BGC context to predicted structures, AF3 QC metrics, compactness descriptors, and Foldseek/PDB structural annotations.

Version and update history

Database version: BGS accession release v1.0. Schema version: public-sqlite-v3. Dataset status: PHRC initial public release with stable BGS accessions. Future HROM integration is planned with the same accession-aware schema.

Identifiers

Each MAG, BGC, primary c0.3 GCF, protein, and predicted structure is assigned a stable BGS accession independent of pipeline-specific identifiers. Original PHRC identifiers are preserved as searchable provenance metadata and in the public accession mapping table.

Scientific workflow

MAG → antiSMASH BGC → primary c0.3 BiG-SCAPE GCF → BGC protein → predicted structure → AF3 QC and Foldseek/PDB annotation where available.

Data availability

Public tables, structures, Foldseek outputs, antiSMASH archives, BiG-SCAPE archives, documentation, and checksums are available through the Downloads page.

Limitations

BGC prediction is computational, structures are predictions, and Foldseek similarity does not prove biological function.

Comparison

BGC Atlas

Broad environmental metagenomic BGC/GCF atlas emphasizing large-scale samples, environments, BGCs, GCFs, filtering, and similarity search.

ABC-HuMi

Human microbiome BGC atlas across body sites emphasizing BGC/GCF/GCC browsing, sequence/BGC query, networks, metadata, and downloads.

PHRC_BGCStructDB

Focused oral microbiome MAG-resolved BGC-protein resource adding protein-level linkage, predicted AF3 structures, AF3 QC, compactness, Foldseek/PDB structural annotation, and downloadable CIF structures.
