Help

MAGs are metagenome-assembled genomes. BGCs are computationally predicted biosynthetic gene clusters. GCFs are BiG-SCAPE gene cluster families.

AlphaFold3 models are predicted structures; pLDDT summarizes local model confidence. Compactness metrics describe broad structural shape and contact density. Foldseek identifies structural similarity to PDB entries, but similarity is not proof of function.

Sequence search is planned for a future BLASTp/MMseqs2 job system and is not active in v1.

Known limitations: computational BGC prediction, predicted structures, missing models for some proteins, missing GCF assignments for some BGCs, PHRC-only initial release, and no public IP prioritization files.
