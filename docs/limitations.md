# Limitations

- BGCs are computational predictions.
- AF3 models are predicted structures, not experimental structures.
- Foldseek/PDB similarity does not establish biological function.
- Some proteins lack predicted CIF structures; many proteins with CIF structures do not yet have parsed AF3 QC or Foldseek/PDB annotations in this release.
- Some BGCs lack a primary GCF assignment.
- PHRC is the initial dataset; HROM support is planned through the existing dataset schema.
- Internal IP prioritization is not part of the public academic resource.
- Full MAG, BGC, GCF, search, and network pages are intentionally deferred until after visual review.
